Support

Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Email idobloch@gmail.com. Please include your device model and the version number shown at the bottom-left of the main menu.

Common questions

How do I play with friends? Tap "Play with friends", type a name and tap "Join the online world". Everyone who joins plays in the same world. Starting the world can take a minute the first time.

The game feels warm or slow. Switch Graphics to "Battery saver" in the main menu or the pause menu.

How do I ride the jet ski, paddleboard or water ski? Walk to the lake shore and use the buttons that appear: Paddleboard, Drive jet ski, or Ski with Tiras.

How do I join the hacky sack circle? Walk up to the circle south-west of the start and tap "Join in". Tap Kick just as the bag reaches you, and aim with the arrows.

How do I delete my data? See the Privacy Policy, or email us.
