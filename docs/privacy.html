Privacy Policy

OpenWorldZero, by Ido Bloch. Last updated October 19, 2026.

OpenWorldZero is a cozy open world to explore with friends and family. We collect as little as possible: only what the game needs to let you play online and pick up where you left off. There are no ads and no advertising tracking, and we never sell or share your data. If you allow it, the game also sends anonymous play data that helps us make it more fun (see below); you can turn that off at any time.

What the game collects

Anonymous player ID. The game signs you in as a guest with Unity Gaming Services. This creates a random player ID (no email, phone number or real name) and a session token stored on your device, so you stay the same player between launches.

Your display name. The name you type in "Play with friends" (up to 16 characters). Other players in the online world can see it above your character. Please don't use your real full name.

Game progress. Your chosen character and where you are in each online world, so you resume exactly where you left off.

Anonymous play data (optional). If you tap OK on the play data note (or leave "Play data" on in the menus), the game records what happens while you play, linked only to a random ID made by the app: which activities you play and for how long, where you are in the game world (about every 5 seconds, as a 10-metre square of the game map), how you join the online world and how long that takes, when the game goes to the background and whether it reconnected, interactions like "Tickle" or "Talk" (not who with), and basic device facts (phone model, operating system, memory, game version and graphics setting). It never includes your name, display name, messages or anything you type. Turn it off any time with "Play data" in the main menu or the pause menu; nothing more is sent after that.

Approximate location from your IP address. When you join the online world, the game looks up your public IP address (through the ipify.org service) only to start the game server in the region nearest to you, for a smooth connection. We don't store your precise location and never use GPS.

What the game does not collect

No contacts, photos, microphone, camera, GPS location, advertising identifiers, health, payment or browsing data. There is no free-text chat: the "Talk" interaction shows preset friendly greetings. Offline play sends nothing except the anonymous play data above, if you allowed it.

How it's used and who processes it

This data is used only to run and improve the game: signing you in, showing your name to the players you play with, saving your progress, connecting you to a nearby server, and (play data) understanding which parts of the game people enjoy and where things go wrong. It is processed by the service providers that run the game for us:

  • Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies): guest sign-in, Cloud Code, saved progress, and storing and analysing the anonymous play data (Cloud Save and Unity Analytics).
  • Edgegap: hosts the online game servers, which see your IP address while you're connected.
  • ipify.org: returns your public IP address so a nearby server can be chosen.

Retention and deletion

Online progress is kept for as long as the game needs it to let you resume. Anonymous play data is kept for up to 13 months. Deleting the app removes the session token from your device; your anonymous player then can't be used again. To ask us to delete your data, email us with your display name and roughly when you played, and we'll remove it.

Children

The game is designed to be friendly for all ages and doesn't ask for personal information. If you believe a child has entered personal information (for example a real full name as display name), contact us and we'll delete it.
