# Open World Zero - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Open World Zero ("the game") is published by Ido Bloch. This policy explains what the Google Play version of the game collects and why.

## What the game collects

- **Guest player ID.** When you open the online features, the game signs you in as an anonymous guest with Unity Gaming Services. This creates a random ID that is stored on your device and on Unity's servers. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Google account.
- **Player name.** If you type a name in the menu, it is stored on your device and, when you play online, sent to the game server and shown above your character to other players in the same world. Do not use your real name if you prefer to stay anonymous.
- **Game progress.** When you play online, the game server stores your character choice and your position in the world under your guest player ID, so you can continue where you left off.
- **Network information.** Like any online service, the game servers (Unity Gaming Services and Edgegap) receive your IP address while you are connected. It is used to pick a nearby server and to run the connection. The game does not keep it.

The game does **not** collect your email, contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera or advertising ID. It has no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

Playing offline sends nothing to any server.

## How the data is used

Only to run the game: signing you in, placing you in an online world, showing your character and name to other players, and saving your progress. The data is not sold and is not shared with anyone except the service providers that host the game:

- Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies) - guest sign-in and world directory
- Edgegap - game server hosting

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Online progress is kept while the world exists. Uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your device, including the guest player ID's sign-in token.

To have your online data deleted, email the address below and include the player name you used. Your data will be deleted within 30 days.
